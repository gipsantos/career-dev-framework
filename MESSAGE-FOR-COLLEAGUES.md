# Message for colleagues (Teams / Email)

## English version

**Subject:** 🚀 Career Development Framework — POC ready for feedback!

---

Hi everyone! 👋

I've been working on a **Career Development Framework** tool — a platform to help team leads structure and track career development within their teams.

It's live as a POC and I'd love your feedback before I move forward with the next iteration.

### 🔗 Try it here: https://career-dev-framework.fly.dev/

### What it does:
- **Self-assessment** — rate your skills 1–5 with evidence from the last 6-12 months
- **Coverage Map** — see team competency gaps at a glance (which skills need more people?)
- **Action Plans** — SMART goals for development
- **Calibration** — team lead can review and calibrate scores during 1:1s
- **AI Skills section** — dedicated section for AI competencies
- **Proficiency tracking** — Entry / Established / Master within each seniority level

### How to test:
1. Open the link above
2. Go to **⚙ Admin** tab → code: `admin2026`
3. Create a team (your name as lead, add a team code)
4. Then login as a team member or as lead to explore the features

### What I need from you:
- **5-10 minutes** of your time to click around
- Your honest feedback on:
  - Is the assessment flow intuitive?
  - Does the coverage map give useful insights?
  - What's missing for YOUR team's reality?
  - Would you use this?

### Planned features (not yet implemented):
- ✏️ Edit existing skills (currently only add/delete)
- 📚 Skill pool shared across teams
- 👥 Multiple leads per team (EM, Product Lead, Domain Lead)
- 📈 Progress charts over time
- 🔑 Proper authentication
- 📊 Management cross-team dashboard

Full roadmap: https://github.com/gipsantos/career-dev-framework/blob/main/ROADMAP.md

### Feedback format (optional structure):
```
Role: [Team Lead / Developer / BA / Architect / EM]
Team size: [number]

What works well:
- ...

What needs improvement:
- ...

Missing features I'd need:
- ...

Would I use this for my team? [Yes / Maybe / No] — Why?
```

Thanks for your time! This is about making career conversations more structured and actionable — not a performance review tool. Your input shapes what comes next. 🙏

— Gisela
