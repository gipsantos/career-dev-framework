## Gap Analysis — Questions to Consider

### For Software Engineer:
- ❓ Missing **Testing** as explicit skill? (unit testing, integration testing, TDD)
- ❓ Missing **Git / Version Control** best practices?
- ❓ Missing **Code Review** (giving and receiving)?
- ❓ Should **Docker / Containerisation** be explicit?
- ❓ Should **Database skills** (SQL, PostgreSQL) be separate from Snowflake?
- ❓ Missing **Incident Response / On-call** behaviour?
- ❓ Should **Estimation & Planning** be in behavioural?

### For Business Analyst:
- ❓ Missing **Wireframing / Prototyping** (Figma)?
- ❓ Missing **Testing / QA collaboration** (UAT, test scenarios)?
- ❓ Missing **Change Management** awareness?
- ❓ Missing **Presentation & Storytelling** skills?
- ❓ Should **Metrics & KPIs** definition be explicit?

### For Architect:
- ❓ Missing **Performance & Scalability** design?
- ❓ Missing **Observability Architecture** (logging, tracing, monitoring design)?
- ❓ Missing **API Design & Governance**?
- ❓ Missing **Technical Debt Management** strategy?
- ❓ Should **Cross-team Collaboration** be explicit?

### Cross-role observations:
- ❓ **Autonomy & Responsibility** — covered implicitly in "Ownership" but could be more explicit per seniority level
- ❓ **Growth Mindset** — not explicitly in any matrix (learning from failure, embracing change)
- ❓ **Time Management & Prioritisation** — relevant for all roles
- ❓ **Continuous Learning** — separate from AI, about staying current in your domain
